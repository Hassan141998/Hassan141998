## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a passionate **Full Stack Developer & AI/ML Engineer** from Pakistan, specializing in building intelligent web applications that solve real-world problems — from healthcare AI to IoT energy systems to fintech tools.

- 🏥 **Healthcare AI:** CardioScan (heart attack prediction) · GlucoseGuard (diabetes classification)
- 💰 **Fintech:** InsureCalc (medical insurance cost prediction with 4 ML models)
- ⚡ **IoT:** Smart Energy Management System with ARIMA forecasting & anomaly detection
- 🎓 **BSCS 2023–2025** · Islamia University of Bahawalpur, Pakistan
- 🌍 Open to **freelance**, **remote**, and **full-time** opportunities
